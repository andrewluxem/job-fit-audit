# Outreach templates

Starting shapes, not scripts. Fill every [bracket] from `profile.md` and real research, and rewrite the first line of every message for the recipient. Tone: direct, specific ask, no filler. Respect the user's search posture: in a quiet search, everything is 1:1 and nothing is posted publicly.

## 1. Warm reconnect
> [Personal opener: the last project or conversation you shared.] News on my end: [one honest line on why you're looking, no drama]. I'm looking at [level] [discipline] roles, [remote / in X]. Not broadcasting it, just reaching out to people whose judgment I trust. Who in your world should I be talking to?

## 2. Referral ask for a specific role
> Quick one: [Company] has a [Role] open and it's a strong fit (it's close to what I ran at [recent employer]). I saw you [work there / worked there until year / know Name]. Would you be comfortable introducing me, or letting me mention your name? I'll send a two-line blurb you can forward, so it's no work on your end.

## 3. The forwardable blurb (send with #2)
Written in third person so the contact can paste it as-is. Two or three sentences, the two strongest verified numbers, one line of range.
> [Name] led [scope] at [Company], where [result with number]. Before that, [second proof point with number] at [Company]. [Years] years across [employers or industries]; strongest at [the one thing that matches this role]. Exploring [Role] at [Company] and worth 20 minutes: [LinkedIn URL]

## 4. Hiring manager (same day as applying)
> [Name], I just applied for the [Role] and wanted to reach you directly rather than sit in the ATS. At [Company] I [top proof point, with number]. Two things I'd look at first at [their company]: [specific idea #1 from their site/app/emails/ads], [specific idea #2]. Worth a 20-minute conversation?

The two ideas ARE the message. Spend real time on their product and marketing to find them. Generic ideas read as spam at senior levels.

## 5. Internal recruiter at a target company
> [Name], I'm a [discipline] leader ([years] years: [2–3 employers], most recently [title] at [company], where I [top result]). [Company] is on my short list because [specific reason]. If there's a current or upcoming [discipline] search, I'd like to be in it. Resume attached.

## 6. Agency recruiter
> I'm a [level] [discipline] candidate in [city], open to [remote/local]. [Years] years at [employers]; strongest at [one or two specifics]; [one proof point or award]. 15 minutes this week to see if I fit any current searches?

## 7. Fractional or consulting pitch (optional bridge income)
> Companies usually call me in one of three moments: [moment 1], [moment 2], [moment 3]. I take [2–3] fractional clients at [hours/week], senior operator who also does the work. Most recently: [result]. Is [Company] in one of those moments, or do you know someone who is?

## Follow-up rules
- No reply isn't no. One nudge at day 5 to 7 with one new line of value; one more at day 14; then park it.
- Thank-you within 24 hours of an interview, referencing one specific thing discussed.
- Report back to anyone who referred you, whatever happens. They vouched; close their loop.
- Log every touch in `pipeline.md` the same day.
