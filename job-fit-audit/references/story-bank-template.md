# Story Bank: [Full Name]

Long-form STAR stories behind the profile's one-line bullets. Used for interview prep, cover-letter hooks, and outreach specifics. Same rule as the profile: if a detail isn't here or in `profile.md`, it doesn't get claimed.

## Rules

1. **Targets are not results.** Mark goals as TARGET. Never convert one into an outcome.
2. **NEEDS DETAIL** marks a story missing its number or its "what I specifically did." Ask the user before it reaches an asset.
3. **Attribution.** The verb attaches to the user's scope; the number attaches to whoever owned it.
4. **Age.** Label each story with its year. Lead with recent stories; use older ones for depth and scale.
